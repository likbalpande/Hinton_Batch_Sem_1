The World Around Us

Explore interesting facts and ideas about people, nature, society, and the world we live in.

Why is education important?

Education helps people develop knowledge, skills, and understanding. It enables individuals to make informed decisions, communicate effectively, and contribute positively to their communities.

What is culture?

Culture refers to the shared traditions, customs, values, beliefs, and ways of life of a particular group or society.

Why are trees important?

Trees provide oxygen, support wildlife, help maintain soil and water resources, and contribute to a healthy environment.

Why is cleanliness important?

Clean surroundings help maintain good health, prevent the spread of diseases, and create a pleasant environment for everyone.

What makes a good citizen?

A good citizen respects others, follows laws and responsibilities, cares for the community, and contributes to the well-being of society.
